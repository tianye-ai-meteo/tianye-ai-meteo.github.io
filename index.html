About

个人简介

PhD candidate at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and the Institute of Atmospheric Physics (IAP), Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research focuses on AI-driven global medium-range weather forecasting, with an emphasis on HEALPix-based spherical models, Swin Transformer architectures, and physics-constrained deep learning to address over-smoothing in medium-range predictions.

中国科学院大学（UCAS）与中国科学院大气物理研究所（IAP, CAS）博士研究生。研究方向为 AI 驱动的全球中期天气预报，重点关注基于 HEALPix 球面网格的模型构建、Swin Transformer 架构，以及引入物理约束的深度学习以缓解中期预报中的过平滑问题。

Research Projects

研究项目

HEALPix-based Global Weather Forecasting

基于 HEALPix 的全球天气预报

Developing a spherical weather forecasting model built on the HEALPix grid, combining Swin Transformer with physics-informed loss functions to improve medium-range forecast skill and reduce over-smoothing.

基于 HEALPix 球面网格构建天气预报模型，融合 Swin Transformer 架构与物理约束损失函数，提升中期预报技巧并缓解过平滑问题。

Physics-Constrained Loss Design

物理约束损失函数设计

Exploring joint physics loss formulations incorporating geostrophic balance and other atmospheric constraints into neural network training for more physically consistent predictions.

探索将地转平衡等大气物理约束以联合损失形式引入神经网络训练，使预测结果在物理上更加一致。
