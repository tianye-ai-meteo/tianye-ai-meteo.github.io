About

个人简介

PhD candidate at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and the Institute of Atmospheric Physics (IAP), Chinese Academy of Sciences. Developed "YanTian" (演天), a global medium-range weather forecasting foundation model achieving world-class forecast skill. My research focuses on Swin Transformer architectures and physics-informed deep learning to improve global medium-range weather forecast accuracy.

中国科学院大学（UCAS）与中国科学院大气物理研究所（IAP, CAS）博士研究生。研发了全球中期天气预报大模型"演天"（YanTian），预报精度达到世界一流水平。研究方向为基于 Swin Transformer 架构、引入物理约束的深度学习提升全球中期天气预报精度。
